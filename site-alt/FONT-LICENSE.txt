The WOFF2 in this directory is a conversion of `atarist-normal.bdf` from that
repository: one square contour per horizontal pixel run, unitsPerEm 16,
advance 8, ascender 14, descender 2. The conversion script is
`scripts/bdf2woff.py`. The outlines, not the bitmaps, are what ship; keep this
notice with the font.
